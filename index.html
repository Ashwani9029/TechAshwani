About Me

An academician shaped by teaching, research and curiosity

Dr. Ashwani Mishra is an Assistant Professor at the School of Pharmacy & Technology Management (SPTM), SVKM'S NMIMS, Indore. Over 19 years, his work has spanned doctoral coursework, postgraduate and undergraduate teaching, and a specialised MSc course in Molecular Modelling & Drug Designing — alongside a research record of more than 101 published papers, 3 patents, and sustained mentorship of postgraduate and undergraduate students.
